About the app

Errant Launcher is a portable launch panel for Windows that keeps its settings and data next to the executable. No installation required: move it to a USB drive or another folder and use it on any computer.

Organize your tools into sections and categories, combine tasks into groups, use search, and customize the look to match your style. Your workspace stays under your control — with nothing to install.

Getting started

  1. Copy ErrantLauncher-x64.exe to any folder — a USB drive works too.
  2. Run the file. On first launch, an ErrantLauncher folder appears next to it — all your data lives there.
  3. Open the Editor from the toolbar and add your first cards — or simply drag files onto the app window.
